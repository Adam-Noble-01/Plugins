Settings

Array Builder 0.2 · Noble Architecture

Live editing

Live Mode updates an open array after you pause typing. Turn it off to prepare changes, then press Update array. Each applied change can be undone in SketchUp.

During path placement the blue wireframe always follows your parameters. Empty or unfinished numbers keep the previous valid preview.

Dimensions accept millimetres, centimetres, metres or inches: 150, 15 cm, 0.15 m or 6 in.

Saved arrays and presets

Array settings and paths travel with the SketchUp model. Component copies remain linked until you edit using Only this array.

Your gallery lives in 05__Data__PresetsLibrary inside the Array Builder project. Updates keep a backup; archived presets remain in 01__Archive.

Previews show object bounding envelopes. Fixed gap mode may overhang the final path point; Fit each segment places the final unit within the segment. Layouts are limited to 5,000 units to keep SketchUp responsive.
