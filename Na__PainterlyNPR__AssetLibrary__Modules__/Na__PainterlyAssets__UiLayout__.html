PainterlyNPR Materials

Click a swatch. Selected faces take it. A selected group or component takes it on every face inside, at every depth, and keeps its glass, so a window's panes survive the frame's swatch. With nothing selected, SketchUp's Paint Bucket picks it up. A component's copies all change together; locked objects and PainterlyNPR assets are left alone.

Each swatch is the materials SSOT's MAT000E__Npr__ material: exempt, so it keeps its colour in every export (whitecard too) and carries its class in the name for ValeVision AURA. Keep them out of ValeVision 3D and TrueVision presentation models.

The Component

Select one component (or group) drawn at its real size, its front facing SketchUp's front (the Front view looks along green, so the front faces away from green). Author moves its origin to the centre of its base, as the library expects.
